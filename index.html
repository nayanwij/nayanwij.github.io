About Me

An infectious disease epidemiologist and biologist with over 10 years of experience, I am passionate about applying data science and statistical methods to understand and prevent infectious diseases. I am currently pursuing my PhD in infectious disease modelling at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine, focusing on Dengue.

I have developed skills in mathematical modelling, spatial analysis, survey design and implementation, database development, and monitoring and evaluation. I've applied these across Sub-Saharan Africa, the Middle East and North Africa, and South & South-East Asia. Most recently, I worked as a Principal Scientist - Epidemiologist at the UK Health Security Agency, where I led analysis of asymptomatic transmission and attack rates.
